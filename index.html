SkiBootLab – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the SkiBootLab Android application (package app.skibootlab, "the App") handles information. The App is developed by Łukasz Dutka ("we", "us").

Summary

  • Camera images are processed only on your device and are never uploaded by the App.
  • Photos you choose to save are stored only on your device.
  • The App does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, location, or contacts.
  • The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage and device statistics.

Camera

The App requests the Camera permission to show a live preview so you can measure the angle of a ski boot. The camera image is processed only on your device. We do not transmit, store on any server, or share camera images.

Photos

When you take a photo, it is saved to your device's gallery in the Pictures/SkiBootLab folder. Photos stay on your device and are not sent to us or any third party by the App. You can delete them at any time using your gallery app.

Settings

Your preferences (for example line color, thickness, and tilt tolerance) are stored locally on your device. They may be included in your device's Android backup, which is governed by Google's terms, not ours.

Analytics (Google Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the App is used and to improve it. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect information such as:

  • an app instance identifier and, where permitted, advertising/device identifiers,
  • device model, operating system version, language, and approximate country/region,
  • app version, first-open and session events, and app usage events,
  • crash/performance-related information.

This information is not used to identify you personally and is not sold. It is transmitted encrypted to Google and processed according to Google's Privacy Policy. More details: Firebase privacy information. Analytics data is retained according to Firebase's default retention settings.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except for the analytics processing by Google described above.

Children

The App is a professional tool for ski-boot fitting and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can revoke the Camera permission at any time in Android settings. You can reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android settings. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored settings.

Data deletion requests

The App has no user accounts. Photos and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete them or uninstall the App. To request deletion of analytics data collected via Firebase, email luk.dutka@gmail.com with the subject "SkiBootLab data deletion". We will process the request within 30 days. Analytics data is otherwise deleted automatically according to Firebase's retention settings.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
